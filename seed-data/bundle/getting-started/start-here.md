# Start Here

Folio is a local-first notebook. Your notes are Markdown files, and Ollama runs the optional language and embedding models on your computer.

## Recommended first steps

1. Open [Set Up Ollama](/getting-started/ollama.md) and check the status indicators in the upper-right corner.
2. Read [Capture and Organize Notes](/getting-started/capture-and-organize.md), then create a small test note.
3. Try the starter task in [Todo List](/todo-list.md).
4. Open [Daily Example](/daily/2026-09-03.md) and add a daily capture of your own.
5. Use [Search, Ask, and Work with Notes](/getting-started/search-ask-and-workspace.md) when you want to find or compare information.

## Where your notes live

The desktop app stores its writable bundle in `~/Documents/Folio`. The Markdown files are the source of truth. Search data can be rebuilt from them with **Reindex** in the Explorer.

These starter guides are ordinary notes. Edit, move, or delete them whenever they are no longer useful.
